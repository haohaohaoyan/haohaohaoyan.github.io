i thought a webOS would be interesting. try:

  • dragging this window by holding on the top bar
  • fullscreen with the green button
  • resizing by dragging the edges of this window
  • open some pages with the shortcuts on the left
  • right clicking, pressing "open file", typing in "assets/start.html", and pressing "open"! this works with all files in this repo, try it out!
